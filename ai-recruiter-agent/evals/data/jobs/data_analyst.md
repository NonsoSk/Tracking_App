# Data Analyst, Growth

Join the growth team to turn product data into decisions.

## What you'll do
- Own weekly growth reporting and the metrics behind it.
- Partner with product managers on launches.

## Requirements
- Advanced SQL for analysing large datasets.
- Building dashboards in Tableau, Looker or Power BI.
- Python or R for analysis (pandas or similar).
- Designing and analysing A/B tests.

## Nice to have
- dbt or data modeling experience.
- Snowflake or BigQuery.
- Presenting insights to non-technical stakeholders.
