How retirement dates are worked out

An employee retires on whichever comes first: reaching the retirement age, or completing the maximum years of service (both configurable in .env). Existing staff IDs are updated; new ones are added.

Expected columns (header names are case-insensitive):

staff_id, first_name, last_name, email, department, job_title, grade, date_of_birth, date_of_employment, status

Department can be the department name or code. Dates can be 2026-05-31 or 31/05/2026.
