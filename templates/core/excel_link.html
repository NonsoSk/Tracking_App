Excel link

The portal follows the recruitment workbooks below and reads new CVs from the CV folder on its own. Each time a workbook is saved with changes, the portal loads it: new rows are added, edited rows are updated, nothing is duplicated, and what you did in the portal is kept. In return, it keeps Portal updates.xlsx beside your workbooks with its latest data.

Good to know

  • Keep the headings in the first row as they are. Sheets are recognised by their headings, so they can be renamed or moved.
  • To follow more files, run link-excel.bat again. The files already linked stay linked, and each is loaded on its own when it changes.
  • Save the workbook as usual. Changes reach the portal a few minutes after you save.
  • Rows you delete in Excel stay in the portal. Remove them in the portal if they should go.
  • A requisition's status follows Excel only when you change its Status cell. Statuses set in the portal are kept otherwise.
  • Once everyone works in the portal, click Stop following.
